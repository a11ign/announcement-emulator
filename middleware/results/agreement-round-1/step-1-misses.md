## Besides the four

- **A page closed while its tree is read was read again with a new converter** (`Target.detachedFromTarget` forgets it), which numbered every node again; the predictor took that for a new document and spoke the page's load and the focus again. Seen in a recording of a fast test through the DevTools source (the root's `uid` went from 1 to 0 between two trees of one page). The converter is now taken before the read. `test_cdp.py` `Converting`.
- **The report** (`supported.ts`, `format.ts`) says the population, date and the fleet's own agreement as the ceiling when the list's figure has them, and the rate of each utterance's shape, with `low confidence` where the list marks it. The list has no figures yet, so nothing prints a rate until step 2 fills it.

## Not done, on purpose

- Matching by the node's **bounds**, as the plan sketched for the extension source: the extension's `location` and the DOM's boxes are in different coordinate frames, so the role-and-name match (the browser's own) is what is used, and the node the tree has focused breaks a tie as before.
- The **sweep over a route change** is deduplicated by the tree's roles and names, so a state that differs only in which links are "same page" is not swept again. That is the sweep's rule (`tree_signature`), not a fault of this step.
